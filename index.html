Core Features

Everything you need to run a modern supply chain

💊

Product Catalog

Suppliers can add, update, and manage medicines with full details — price, batch, expiry, and manufacturer info.

📦

Inventory Management

Real-time stock level tracking with automatic low-stock alerts and medicine expiry date monitoring.

🛒

Bulk Order Management

Pharmacies can browse, add to cart, and place bulk orders with full order history and status tracking.

🚚

Delivery Tracking

Live order tracking from Pending → Shipped → Delivered. Suppliers update status, pharmacies stay informed.

💳

Payment & Invoicing

Supports online payments and credit-based transactions. Invoices are auto-generated for every order.

📊

Reports & Dashboards

Role-specific dashboards with sales reports, inventory summaries, and real-time system overviews.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Automated alerts for order updates, low stock warnings, and medicine expiry reminders.

👥

Role-Based Access

Separate portals for Pharmacy, Supplier, and Admin with strict access control per user role.

How It Works

From registration to delivery, in four steps

01

Register & Verify

Sign up as a Pharmacy, Supplier, or Admin. Your account is verified and role-based access is granted.

02

Browse & Order

Pharmacies browse the live medicine catalog, search by category, and add products to their cart.

03

Pay & Confirm

Place bulk orders, complete payment online or via credit, and receive an auto-generated invoice.

04

Track & Receive

Track your order in real time. Suppliers update shipping status as the order moves to delivery.

User Roles

Pharmacy Portal

Access the full medicine catalog, manage your inventory, and place bulk orders with ease.

  • Browse & search medicine catalog
  • Add to cart & place bulk orders
  • View order history & status
  • Receive stock & expiry alerts

Manage your product listings, track orders from pharmacies, and oversee your inventory in real time.

  • Manage all user accounts
  • Monitor system activity
  • View platform-wide reports
  • Ensure regulatory compliance
  • Audit logs & security oversight
